## Detection

- **RDS on detected FM broadcast stations** (done 2026-10-11, untested on air: no FM capture): `detect::rds` (pilot-locked 57 kHz BPSK, block sync, burst correction, PI → call sign, PS, RT, PTY, AF, CT) and `engine::rds_monitor` (one station at a time per live source, revisit every 10 s) put the station on its occupant chip (`FM broadcast · KXYZ "PS"`). Left: RT/PTY in the inspector; HD Radio PSD once HD decode exists.
- **ATSC / ATSC 3.0 programme data.** ATSC 1.0 is 8-VSB in 6 MHz, so an R2 at 10 MS/s sees a whole channel; channel names and the programme guide live in the PSIP tables (VCT, EIT, ETT) of the 19.4 Mb/s transport stream, which means a full 8-VSB receiver (timing, equaliser, trellis + RS decoding) before any table can be read: feasible in software (GNU Radio has one) but CPU-heavy and a big piece of work. ATSC 3.0 is OFDM with LDPC; the bootstrap and L1 signalling are open and would at least identify the service, but service lists (SLT over ROUTE/IP) need most of a 3.0 receiver, and no mature open demodulator exists.
- **LTE / NR cell identification** (done 2026-10-11, synthetic tests only): `detect::lte` does cyclic-prefix numerology (15/30 kHz), PSS/SSS (PCI, timing, CFO) and the MIB (bandwidth, SFN, 1 or 2 antenna ports, soft-combined over 4 frames); `engine::cell_monitor` checks each cellular occupant in a live span every 20 s (120 s once identified) and the chip reads e.g. "LTE 10 MHz PCI 217 (2 ports)". Left: NR PSS/SSS (numerology only today), 4-port MIB, thresholds on air, a per-market licensee table in `bandplan` for the operator; SIB1 not planned.
- **GSM detector** (done 2026-10-11, synthetic tests only; nothing on air here): `detect::gsm` finds the FCCH tone bursts (CFO), decodes the SCH (BSIC, frame number) and the classifier labels 150–300 kHz channels "GSM BCCH BSIC n-m".
- **Auto-squelch flashes on wideband signals** (done 2026-10-11, watch on air): a known occupant's allowed misses grow with its sightings (3 + up to 12 looks) and its extent is the union of its last four cuts.
- **HD Radio (NRSC-5) decode.** The hybrid digital sidebands are already detected in Focus; decoding them (OFDM sync, layer 1 FEC, HDC audio) is a large piece of work and the audio codec is proprietary, so start with station/programme metadata (SIS) and see how far the open descriptions go.
- **Decoding next steps.** P25 NID (NAC/DUID, BCH-corrected), DMR slot type (colour code/data type, Golay-corrected) and NXDN LICH are decoded and checked against real captures (`detect/tests/real_captures.rs`). Next: P25 TSBK (control channel messages: system/site IDs, grants → follow voice), DMR CSBK, DMR EMB (colour code on voice bursts), NXDN SACCH/RAN, P25 Phase 2. Also confirm the NXDN direction bit against a known transmitter.
- **Per-protocol re-slicing.** The prefilter cutoff is chosen before the protocol is known (4–6.5 kHz). Weak DMR improved further at 4 kHz (22 → ~50 slot types), so re-slice with a protocol-specific cutoff once identified.
- **Features are weak on real signals.** On hardware, FSK level counts and symbol rates often come out blank even where the sync bank confirms a protocol (NXDN48 at 460 MHz with ×8–18 syncs, DMR at 855.59 with ×15–33). Deviation also reads high (noise, discriminator clicks). Once a sync is found, use its timing to slice symbols properly (levels set per sync) and measure levels/deviation from that, instead of a raw histogram.
- **TDMA single-slot traffic fragments into many short records** (e.g. 47 records in 45 s at 856.3375), each too short to hold two syncs, so they stay unidentified. Group records by channel/emitter and classify the group, or lengthen the hangover for slotted signals.
- **Unverified labels:** ProVoice (binary sync, 2-bit tolerance) seen ×3 at 854.71 MHz, and the X2-TDMA patterns. Treat as provisional until checked against a known transmitter.

- **Spectral identifier / squelch, open items** (`detect::spectral`, `engine::occupancy`; regression in `detect/tests/real_spectra.rs`):
  - A faint flat TV channel without a pilot (ch 35, 596–602 MHz, +4.8 dB between two ATSC 1.0 neighbours; likely ATSC 3.0) isn't found: the flat-block test needs ≥ 3 dB with ≤ 3 dB ripple and an edge dip.
  - LTE needs ≥ 20 MHz in view (a Focus span can sit inside a carrier with no floor to judge by); Focus panes rely on the survey's list. Uplink bands aren't identified (bursty).
  - Survey FM detection at 54 kHz bins works; HD sidebands need ≤ 15 kHz bins (Focus).
  - Known occupants are kept in memory per source; persisting them per location would let a fresh start squelch at once.
- **Carriers vs spurs** (`detect::carrier`, `detect::context`): DC, the radio's 5 MHz spur grid, the USB-transfer tone on internal spurs (152.6 Hz at 10 MS/s), CW birdies (pure, steady lines) and CTCSS tones are labelled. An airband birdie comb at 200 kHz spacing (119.6, 119.8, 122.2–122.8 MHz) is sometimes still called "Aviation AM" when the line is weak; detecting the comb itself (equal spacing) would settle it.
- **ISM bands:** LoRa chirp detection (`detect::chirp`) is only tested on synthetic chirps: 433.92 MHz was silent for 90 s and nothing at 902–928 chirped. 902–928 here is ~155 ms narrow bursts hopping between 904.99, 905.79, 910.60, 914.80 and 915.80 MHz with ±25 kHz sidelines (likely FHSS meters); grouping hops across emitters would name them.
- **Talk activity:** DMR voice/control/data from slot types and voice syncs, talkgroup/source from voice LC headers (BPTC + RS(12,9)), CSBK opcodes (Cap+ system status confirmed by CRC on a real capture); P25 from DUIDs, with the talkgroup from control-channel grants matched by frequency; NXDN from the LICH. P25 voice link control (`detect::p25_lc`: TDULC Golay + RS, LDU1 Hamming + RS) names talkgroup and radio on traffic and conventional channels; checked against control-channel grants recorded at the same time (`testdata/captures/P25_site_*`). Vendor link control (MFID > 1, ~20% of words here) isn't parsed. P25 Phase 2 (`detect::p25p2`): superframe fragments, ISCH, DUID, descrambling (WACN/system from the Phase 1 control channel via the engine's monitor; NAC from Phase 1 NIDs or Phase 2 end-of-PTT), RS(63,35) with punctured symbols as erasures, MAC PTT / group / unit voice → talkgroup and radio. ~89% of scrambled FACCH decode on the local site's three TDMA channels (`testdata/captures/P25P2_site_*`). Without the control channel in view, scrambled bursts stay undecoded (only unscrambled end-of-PTT talkgroups). Other MAC opcodes (0x42, 0x7C, ...) aren't parsed. Next: DMR embedded LC on voice bursts (needs DMR voice to test on; none heard locally yet). Weak DMR slicing is much better since the per-sync slicer (41 of 58 CSBKs pass on the 855.59 capture, was 4 of 20).
- **Pagers** (`detect::flex`, `detect::pocsag`, pager monitors in `engine::monitor`, Pages tab):
  - FLEX 3200/4 decodes on real signal (929.6125 MHz). The 1600/2, 1600/4 and 3200/2 speeds are implemented from the spec but untested: nothing local sends them.
  - About half the blocks on 929.6125 are collisions with a second, GPS-aligned co-channel FLEX transmitter (another zone or system): headers decode, data doesn't, and pages come out spliced. Timing now comes from sync 2, data is read through a quarter-symbol filter and 2-bit corrections are only trusted in fully decoding blocks (2026-10-10). Left: a tone filter bank with soft decisions for the moderately interfered blocks, and per-block interference erasure so collided stretches show as gaps rather than the other site's words.
  - Long FLEX messages split across frames (done 2026-10-11): `detect::flex_join::FragmentJoiner` in the pager monitor joins fragments by capcode and fragment number, marks a missing fragment with " … ", and releases an incomplete message after 8 frames. On the four captures every fragment was an orphan (the other half fell in a collided frame), so a live join is untested. Noted: the decoder reports a frame every ~10 s in the 60 s / 120 s captures, not every 1.875 s; idle frames or undetected ones?
  - POCSAG is only tested on synthetic signals (none heard locally).
  - The odd 120 s FLEX capture (frames 1.863 s apart instead of 1.875 s) was never explained; fresh captures are fine.
- **Capacity Plus trunking** (DMR, `dmr::CapPlusStatus`, Cap+ link control):
  - Decoded: talkgroup / radio / voice channel from Cap+ group voice link control; emitters learn their own repeater number from it ("CC 9 · Cap+ rep 3"). Untested on real voice: none heard yet.
  - System status "rest channel" field (SDRTrunk's layout) is unconfirmed: on the local site (855.3125, 855.5875, 856.3375, 856.9125 MHz) it climbs 3 → 7 over a day, one step every several minutes, the same on every repeater, always slot 2, with no traffic. Timed rest rotation, or a different field. Recordings: `testdata/captures/CapPlus_site_*` (10 min, all four at once).
  - The status activity bytes (which repeaters are busy, with which talkgroups) aren't decoded: every status heard so far was from an idle site. Needs a capture during traffic (a weekday morning?).
  - Next: a per-site repeater → frequency table from the learned numbers, then label busy channels with talkgroups and follow calls.
- **NXDN control messages** (CAC/SACCH): need the NXDN Common Air Interface spec (Part 1-A) for the interleave, puncturing and CRC details; the NXDN Forum's download needs a login.
- **Call log and trunk following** (`engine::calls`, Calls tab): grants and voice-channel signalling merge per call; while a grant is being re-announced the classifier samples its voice channel every 2 s (~100 ms each), ahead of other work. On air: 77/80 granted calls heard and named in one 4-minute run, ~40% in another (weak channels such as 774.41875 often read as analog FM here). Durations are from first to last sighting. Not yet: DMR/Cap+ grants (no Cap+ activity decoded), grants for channels outside the span (shown granted-only), per-call audio.
- **Digital voice: IMBE (P25 Phase 1)** (`detect::imbe`, `detect::mbe`), written from TIA-102.BABA (tables in `imbe_tables.rs` generated from the document and cross-checked: block lengths sum to L, every L spends 73 − K bits). Voice frames come from every LDU (`p25_voice_frames` recovers LDUs the sync search missed from their 864-symbol spacing: 114 → 178 on the weakest capture); LDU2 encryption sync (ALGID/KID) and LDU1 service options mark encrypted calls, which stay silent (ADP key 288 seen locally). Checked against mbelib and OP25 run out of tree on identical frames: levels within ~15% of OP25 on normal frames. Departure from BABA: repeated frames fade (½ per repeat, silent after 3) instead of buzzing. Soft decision (Chase decoding of each Golay/Hamming word over the slicer's soft symbols, 5 least reliable bits) is on: on synthetic noise it loses a third fewer frames than hard decision; on the local captures it cuts reserved pitch codes by ~37% but pitch continuity is unchanged, so the slicer's soft values are probably not well calibrated yet. Phase 2 frames are still hard-decision. Output is at the spec's level (quiet): playback applies gain.
- **Live P25 voice and call following** (`detect::p25_voice::VoiceStream`, `engine::listen`): Listen → P25 mode plays a Phase 1 channel; "Follow calls" plays whatever the control channel grants (newest clear Phase 1 grant in the span, held while voice keeps coming, ~1 s behind live). Encrypted transmissions stay silent; a talkgroup heard encrypted is skipped for 10 minutes. On air (5 min): clear TG 10064/10002 transmissions recorded and named; ADP calls (TG 7502, 10012, 10044) detected. Recordings play inside the app (▶ in the Calls tab; right-click opens the system player). Talkgroup priority and lockout (right-click a call in the Calls tab; saved in `Documents/SpecTrawl/follow.txt`): a priority grant interrupts a call on any other talkgroup, a locked-out talkgroup is never followed; checked on air (lockout of TG 10064 held for 3 min; TG 48015 priority preempted a TG 10032 call).
- **Digital voice: P25 Phase 2 (half-rate, AMBE+2)** (`detect::ambe`, `p25p2` voice bursts, `p25_voice::Phase2Stream`), written from TIA-102.BABA-1 (tables generated and cross-checked). Voice frames from 4V/2V bursts, descrambled with the system's sequence (picked per burst by Golay errors); MAC_PTT's ALGID and the grant's service options mark encrypted calls. Synthesis matches mbelib's 2450 decoder (run out of tree) in spectrogram. Following now takes Phase 2 grants on their timeslot; manual listening plays whichever timeslot starts talking. On air (5 min): Phase 2 TG 47159/48015 and Phase 1 TG 10064 followed and recorded. Not yet: tone frames are synthesized but untested on air; Encryption sync (ESS: four ESS-B pieces from the 4V bursts + ESS-A from the 2V burst, RS(44,16), ALGID/KID/MI) is read from the voice itself, so a call joined after its PTT is still recognised as encrypted within a superframe (checked against MAC_PTT on `P25P2_site_769.1885`: ADP key 9828). Patent note: AMBE+2 half-rate (US 8,359,197) runs to May 2028; keep software AMBE+2 out of any commercial build before then.
- **Broadcast FM fragments in Focus.** The narrowband detector cuts a ±75 kHz station into pieces (4–98 kHz wide, centres ±20 kHz) that become 4–9 emitters per station (99.5, 99.9, 100.7, 101.5 MHz in one export). Now labelled and squelched as the known station; the fragments still exist underneath.
- **Bandwidth of FM broadcast/voice varies with modulation**, so a record's extent is the union over its lifetime. Fine for now; a per-row occupied-bandwidth average would be steadier.
- **Wideband signals that fill a floor block** (e.g. an LTE carrier filling most of a 10 MHz focus span) raise the floor estimate and can go undetected. Needs a floor that also looks at neighbouring blocks or history.

## Hardware

- **Manual Airspy gain stages** (done 2026-10-11): Gain → Manual gives LNA, mixer and VGA sliders (AGC off) through `GainSetting::Manual`; the hop profile is keyed by it. Untested on hardware tonight.
- **tinySA Ultra support** (on hand). A swept spectrum analyzer, not an SDR: no IQ, so no look-back or decoding. Three uses:
  - **Calibration reference:** reads in dBm, so measuring the same signal on both gives per-device dBFS→dBm offsets for the Airspys.
  - **Test source:** its signal-generator output gives known tones to measure Airspy gain tables and passband shape, and to test detection repeatably.
  - **Survey outside the R2's range:** about 100 kHz–6 GHz (HF below 24 MHz; 1.8–6 GHz for cellular, Wi-Fi, drone links).
  - Needs a second kind of source, a *power-sweep device* producing spectrum rows directly (USB serial text protocol). The Survey role and burst detector already work on rows. Check its COM port and firmware commands before writing it.
- **R2s hear each other's local oscillator** (partly done 2026-10-11): the survey blanks each focus Airspy's centre + 5 MHz ± 15 kHz as it retunes (`Source::set_masks`), besides hiding survey detections inside focus spans. Left: the weaker spur offsets (measure them), and the attenuation/filter suggestion.
- **Two R2s on one USB bus interfere.** With both R2s sharing a hub, the sweeping one put a dense comb of narrow carriers into the other (856.6–859.5 MHz). On separate buses it's gone. Worth a note in docs and maybe a warning when two Airspys share a hub.
- **A second Airspy sometimes can't be opened while the first streams** (`AIRSPY_ERROR_NOT_FOUND`), seen in the `classify_sim` example but not in the app. libairspy opens each device in turn to read serials; the exact trigger isn't known yet.
- **Recover when a device is unplugged** (done 2026-10-11, untested on hardware): a lost device is rescanned every 5 s and reopened into the same slot with its tuning when its serial is back; the pane title says DEVICE LOST meanwhile.
